  <section>
    <h2>Course Details</h2>
    <p>This study introduces major ideas in quantum algorithms, together with some discussion of quantum complexity. It focuses on the basic building blocks of quantum algorithms and how these components are combined to form larger algorithmic frameworks.</p>
    <p>It will cover several important algorithmic techniques and help participants build a broad picture of how quantum algorithms work and where quantum speedups may arise.</p>
    <dl class="course-facts">
      <dt>Goals</dt>
      <dd>
        <ul>
          <li>Develop intuition for quantum algorithms and quantum complexity.</li>
          <li>Become familiar with several important quantum algorithmic techniques.</li>
          <li>Gain a sense of which computational tasks may benefit from quantum algorithms.</li>
        </ul>
      </dd>
      <dt>Plan</dt>
      <dd>8 weeks.</dd>
    </dl>
  </section>
