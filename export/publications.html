Peer-reviewed articles

2025
M. Lee, M. Shin, J. Lee, K. Jeong.
Mutual information maximizing quantum generative adversarial networks.
Nature Scientific Reports 15(1), 32835.

We propose training a quantum generator with a mutual-information-maximizing objective rather than fidelity. The bound is implemented via a disentangling QNN. We evaluate the model on synthetic distributions and on a financial return series benchmark, where it converges with fewer parameters than comparable baselines.

M. Shin, S. Lee, J. Lee, M. Lee, D. Ji, H. Yeo, K. Jeong.
Disentangling quantum neural networks for unified estimation of quantum entropies and distance measures.
Physical Review A 110, 062418.

A single disentangling ansatz, trained once, supports estimators for von Neumann entropy, quantum relative entropy, and trace distance — without the protocol switching usually required to estimate each separately.
